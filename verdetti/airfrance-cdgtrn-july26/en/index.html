THE VERDICT

7

Air France remains a solid, premium choice for the inflight experience, thanks to its elegant cabins, comfortable aircraft, and impeccable onboard service. However, the overall journey is heavily penalized by significant ground logistics inefficiencies, including rude staff at the Turin station, suboptimal online services, and the structural inconvenience of Terminal 2G at Paris Charles de Gaulle.

Pros
  • Modern and quiet aircraft
  • Elegant and well-maintained cabin
  • Attentive and professional onboard service
  • Great overall seating comfort
Cons
  • Rude ground service at Turin (TRN)
  • Substandard, slow online platforms
  • Slow and unreliable customer support
  • Inconvenient logistics at Terminal 2G (CDG)
